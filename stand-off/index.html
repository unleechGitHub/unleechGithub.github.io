05 / A LITTLE HELP

Before you begin.

I can’t see “Join group.”

Sign in to Google with your Google Play account, then reopen the group. Check the About page. If you still cannot join, email us with the Google account you want added.

Is the game free?

Stand Off: Enchanted is a paid game. This alpha offers free test access to registered license testers. A test purchase is not a promise of a free copy after testing ends.

Do I need an internet connection?

You can play offline. Friend Duel, cloud saves, the Weekly Trial and leaderboards require an internet connection. Signing up and downloading also require internet access.

Which platforms can I test?

This signup is for the Android closed alpha on Google Play. Other platform announcements will be shared when ready.

The testing page says I’m not eligible.

Check that you joined the group and opened the testing invitation with the same account. Country availability and device compatibility can also affect access. Contact us with the exact message and your device model.

Where do I report a bug privately?

Email sirunleech@gmail.com. Include the game version, device model, steps to reproduce, and any useful screenshots. Do not include passwords or payment details.
