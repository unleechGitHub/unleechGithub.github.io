Privacy policy

Stand Off: Enchanted · effective 1 October 2026 · crash reports and optional statistics added 1 October 2026 · optional statistics extended 8 October 2026

Stand Off: Enchanted is a fantasy duel game. We collect what its online features need to work, crash reports so we can fix bugs, and gameplay statistics only if you agree. We do not sell your data, and the game shows no ads.

Questions or requests: sirunleech@gmail.com.

Your settings, saved duels and the rest of the game's local data stay on your device. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, messages, payment details or advertising ID.

Who processes it

The online features run on Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Leaderboards, Relay and Lobby, Diagnostics (crash reports) and Analytics (the optional statistics). Unity processes this data on our behalf to provide those services, and, like any online service, receives your device's IP address when the game connects. See Unity's privacy policy. The game is distributed through Google Play, which delivers the game and its optional downloads (such as voice packs) under Google's privacy policy. Unity's servers may be in a country other than yours.

Who can see it

  • Other players see your display name and scores on the leaderboards, may meet your completed Trial build (with your display name) as a Rival Echo, and see your display name and hero in a friend duel.
  • Names other players can see are checked against a list of offensive words, in the game and on our server; a refused name is shown as "Ascendant".
  • We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your online data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your cloud save, scores, shared build, run records and the account itself are deleted. Two exceptions: past weekly leaderboards are archived by the leaderboard service when each week ends and can no longer be changed, so a name and score from an earlier week may remain there; and a copy of your shared build that another player's game already fetched stays on their device until it is replaced. Our service providers may keep data in backups for a limited time before it is erased.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data at any time: in the game (Profile, Manage Save Data, Delete All) or by email. See how to delete your account.
  • Play offline: Vs AI, the Journey and the Trials work without a connection. When the game is online, your cloud save (Android) keeps itself up to date automatically.
  • Gameplay statistics are off unless you agree. Switch them off at any time in Profile (Statistics); this stops them and asks Unity to delete what you shared.
  • Crash reports have no on/off switch, because we need them to keep the game working; they are kept for 90 days, then erased, and cannot be deleted one by one before that. If you shared statistics and no longer have the game, email us the installation ID shown on the Delete All screen (Profile, Manage Save Data, Delete All) and we will have them deleted.
  • Leave the display name unset to appear as "Ascendant" and a number.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Security

All data the game sends online is encrypted in transit. Leaderboard scores are checked by our server before they are posted.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has used the online features, email us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will be described here before they take effect in the game.
