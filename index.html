How a duel plays

Victory isn’t luck. It’s reading your rival.

  1. 1Choose your spell

    Six schools of magic, plus Guard and Charge. Every spell costs mana, and every number is on the table.

  2. 2Read your rival

    You both choose at the same time. Only their next move is hidden: guess what they will do, and pick what beats it.

  3. 3Win the clash

    The spells meet and the stronger one wins. Fire has the edge over Grass, Grass over Water, and Water over Fire.

Think rock-paper-scissors with mana, open numbers and effects that last: every choice is a read, not a guess.

Strategic depth

Simple to learn. Challenging to master.

The first duel takes a minute to understand. The next hundred keep finding new answers.

Six schools of magic

  • Fire
  • Water
  • Grass
  • Light
  • Void
  • Earth

Each one has its own strength and its own counter.

How they work

Fire has the edge over Grass, Grass over Water, and Water over Fire. Light cleanses or shields you, Void drains your rival’s mana, and Earth breaks through a Guard.

Mana is everything

Build it, guard it, and spend it when it matters most.

Guard and Charge

Bigger spells cost more mana. Charge builds the mana they need; Guard blunts an attack. Spend too early and your rival will know you are empty.

Master Convergence

Cast three schools to ready a devastating finishing spell.

The finisher

Convergence carries the effect of every sigil you earned on the way to it, so the schools you cast first shape what it does.

Expect the unexpected

Arcana rewrite the rules of a duel and make you rethink your plan.

The Arcane Veto

The Arcane Veto deals Arcana that change the duel. Reject the ones that hurt you, or bank a seal for later. There are 45 Arcana to discover.

Read the battlefield

The sky and lasting effects shape the clashes still to come.

Skies and effects

Sunny, Rainy and Full Moon skies each strengthen a school. Burn, Weaken, Seeded, Ward and Stoneguard carry from one clash to the next.

Two ways to duel

One spell at a time, or three locked in and revealed together.

Classic and Tactical

Classic duels resolve one spell at a time. Tactical duels lock in three spells and reveal them together, so you plan a whole sequence against your rival’s.

Every rule, all 45 Arcana and every hero’s passive are in the Grimoire.

Twelve duelists

Every duelist bends one rule.

Each hero and rival carries a class passive. It triggers on its own condition, then recharges for three clashes. Hover over or tap an emblem to see it move, as it does in the game.

  • Wizard

    Elemental Mastery

    When your Fire, Water or Grass beats the rival's spell, it gains +1 more PWR.

  • Lich

    Soul Siphon

    After your spell deals damage, restore 1 HP.

  • Footman

    Stalwart Guard

    When your Guard meets an attack, gain 1 MP.

  • Hellfire Devil

    Hellfire

    After a Fire spell deals damage, your next offensive spell gains +1 PWR.

  • Thornfury Devil

    Thornfury

    After a Grass spell deals damage, your next offensive spell gains +1 PWR.

  • Riptide Devil

    Riptide

    After a Water spell deals damage, your next offensive spell gains +1 PWR.

  • Ironwall Guard

    Hold the Line

    When you Guard and a spell still deals you damage, take 1 less.

  • Counterguard

    Counterguard

    When you Guard and a spell still deals you damage, your next offensive spell gains +1 PWR.

  • Aegis Guard

    Aegis Reserve

    When your Guard meets an attack, gain Ward against the next status.

  • Gravechill Ghoul

    Grave Chill

    After taking spell damage, your next offensive spell restores 1 MP if it deals damage.

  • Carrion Bloom Ghoul

    Carrion Bloom

    After taking spell damage, your next offensive spell restores 1 HP if it deals damage.

  • Grave Hunger Ghoul

    Grave Hunger

    After taking spell damage, your next offensive spell restores 1 HP and 1 MP if it deals damage.
