How a duel plays

The numbers are open. The next choice isn’t.

I

Read your rival

Both duelists choose at the same time, then the spells clash. Higher power wins. Guard blunts an attack, and Charge builds the mana your bigger spells need.

Classic duels resolve one spell at a time. Tactical duels lock in three and reveal them together.

II

Six schools of magic

  • Fire
  • Water
  • Grass
  • Light
  • Void
  • Earth

Fire has the edge over Grass, Grass over Water, and Water over Fire. Light cleanses or shields you, Void drains your rival’s mana, and Earth breaks through a Guard.

Cast three schools to ready Convergence, a finishing spell that carries the effect of every sigil you earned.

III

Rules that shift

The Arcane Veto deals Arcana that rewrite the rules of the duel. Reject the ones that hurt you, or bank a seal for later. There are 45 Arcana to discover.

The sky changes too: Sunny, Rainy and Full Moon skies each strengthen a school. Burn, Weaken, Seeded, Ward and Stoneguard carry from one clash to the next.

Twelve duelists

Every duelist bends one rule.

Each hero and rival carries a class passive. It triggers on its own condition, then recharges for three clashes. Hover over or tap an emblem to see it move, as it does in the game.

  • Wizard

    Elemental Mastery

    When your Fire, Water or Grass beats the rival's spell, it gains +1 more PWR.

  • Lich

    Soul Siphon

    After your spell deals damage, restore 1 HP.

  • Footman

    Stalwart Guard

    When your Guard meets an attack, gain 1 MP.

  • Hellfire Devil

    Hellfire

    After a Fire spell deals damage, your next offensive spell gains +1 PWR.

  • Thornfury Devil

    Thornfury

    After a Grass spell deals damage, your next offensive spell gains +1 PWR.

  • Riptide Devil

    Riptide

    After a Water spell deals damage, your next offensive spell gains +1 PWR.

  • Ironwall Guard

    Hold the Line

    When you Guard and a spell still deals you damage, take 1 less.

  • Counterguard

    Counterguard

    When you Guard and a spell still deals you damage, your next offensive spell gains +1 PWR.

  • Aegis Guard

    Aegis Reserve

    When your Guard meets an attack, gain Ward against the next status.

  • Gravechill Ghoul

    Grave Chill

    After taking spell damage, your next offensive spell restores 1 MP if it deals damage.

  • Carrion Bloom Ghoul

    Carrion Bloom

    After taking spell damage, your next offensive spell restores 1 HP if it deals damage.

  • Grave Hunger Ghoul

    Grave Hunger

    After taking spell damage, your next offensive spell restores 1 HP and 1 MP if it deals damage.

Help shape the game

Join the Android alpha

Four steps, in order. Use the same Google account you use on Google Play for every step.

Join before you install

The game is also in early access on Google Play, where anyone can buy it. Alpha testers who join here get a free test copy, but only if steps 1 to 3 are done before you install. Install first and Google Play charges the normal price.

  1. Sign in to Google

    Sign in with the Google account you use on Google Play on your Android device. The next steps only work for that account.

  2. Join the tester group

    Open the group and select Join group. Just opening the page doesn’t make you a member, and this website can’t see or confirm whether you joined.

    Open the tester group ↗

    groups.google.com/g/soe-alpha-test

  3. Become a tester

    After joining the group, open the closed-testing invitation and select Become a tester.

    Open the testing invitation ↗

    play.google.com/apps/testing/com.unleech.standoff

  4. Install from Google Play

    Only once steps 1 to 3 are done, open the game’s Play Store listing and install. Group members are set up as license testers, so no promo code is needed.

    At checkout, tap the payment method and choose Test card, always approves. It’s a test card, so you won’t be charged. Don’t pick “always declines” or “approves then charges back”: the first fails, and the second takes the game away again.

    Open Google Play ↗

    play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.unleech.standoff

If a price appears

Testers see the Test card payment methods, or a checkout that says it’s a test purchase or that you won’t be charged. Only continue in that case. If you only see your own cards or an ordinary paid checkout, stop: that is the normal early access purchase. Check that you used the same account for every step, wait a few minutes after joining, then try again or contact us.

Already paid by mistake? Ask Google Play for a refund from your order history, or email us.

What makes a report easy to fix

  • What you were doing: the mode (a Journey stage, a Trials floor, VS AI…) and your class.
  • What happened, and what you expected instead.
  • Whether it happens again when you repeat the same steps.
  • Your device model and the game version.
  • A screenshot or a short screen recording, if you can.

Never include passwords or payment details.

Troubleshooting

If something isn’t working

I can’t see “Join group”

Make sure you’re signed in to Google with your Google Play account, then reopen the group. If you still can’t join, email support@standoffenchanted.fyi with the Google account you want added.

The testing page says I’m not eligible

Check that you joined the group and opened the invitation with the same account. Country availability and device compatibility can also affect access. Contact us with the exact message and your device model.

Google Play says the app can’t be found

Check that you’re signed in to Google Play with the same account you used for the group and the invitation, then search again in a few minutes. If it still doesn’t appear, your country or device may not be supported yet: email us your device model and country.

Google Play says my device isn’t compatible

The alpha is for Android phones and tablets on Google Play, and some low-memory devices aren’t supported. Tell us your device model so we can check.

Checkout shows a real price

Tap the payment method. If Test card, always approves is listed, choose it and continue: you won’t be charged. If you only see your own cards, that’s the paid early access checkout: don’t complete it if you meant to join the free alpha. Confirm you joined the group and accepted the invitation with the account signed in to Google Play, wait a few minutes, then try again or contact us. Never share payment details in the group or by email.

Is the game free?

Stand Off: Enchanted is a paid game. Players who join early access on Google Play buy it. Alpha testers who join through this page get free test access as registered license testers. A test purchase isn’t a promise of a free copy after testing ends.

Which platforms can I test?

This signup is for the Android closed alpha on Google Play. Other platforms will be announced when they’re ready.
