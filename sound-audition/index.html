Duel Sound Audition

Real in-game recordings of the Enchanted duel: music, ambience, casts, effects, contact and voice lines, exactly as the duel presents each clash. Every pair uses the same clash, seed and volume (all settings at 100%), so the only difference is the sound work.

What the audit found

Measured from the clips and from these recordings, before changing anything.

Contact vs. the cast before itContact 4–9 dB quieter than the castAbout level on average; most hits now above the cast
ProcsUp to three sounds, a beat apartUp to two quiet textures with the contact; one signature (Convergence ready, Phoenix) after it
  • The cast layer was the loudest part of most clashes: the projectile trail prefabs loop their own audio at full volume during flight, and some spell prefabs fire an explosion sound when they spawn. In the Enchanted duel those now sit well under the contact; Classic is unchanged.
  • Lethal hits and Convergence impacts dip the music and ambience briefly instead of getting louder.
  • Musical tones are reserved for the Convergence's three-note motif. The Boon proc is now a tiny glint, and Cleanse and Phoenix have no bells.
  • Fixed on the way: effect prefabs replayed from the pool got quieter on every reuse whenever SFX volume was below 100%.
